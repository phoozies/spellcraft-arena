# Controls

You cast a spell by **drawing its shape**: the moment you finish, it flies where you are looking. There is no cast button, and spells can't be stored. The shape is the kind of move: circle = a basic attack, a diagonal slash = a fast attack, a square = a boost, a triangle = a defense or a stun, a lightning bolt = a heavy attack, a V = an area attack, a star = your ultimate; chest spells use a heart, a zigzag, a hook, an X, or a spiral. **Key N (1-5) and card N are the Nth spell of your loadout.** **You can only draw in the Training Groves (hub, west and east sides), during a match's rounds, and in the Battleground**, not elsewhere in the hub and not during a match's intro, countdown, draft, or results. The key that opens a blank drawing canvas (default **F**) can be changed in **Settings**. The setting is saved to your profile, and keys used for movement, chat, the spell keys, canceling (**G**), rotating (**R**), dashing (**Q**), or the Admin panel (**`**) can't be chosen. You can draw one spell per second. The spell cards at the bottom of the screen, the element shrines in the hub, and the Spellbook all show what to draw.

## Drawing tips

- **You draw with your wand.** While the canvas is open players near you see your wand raised and a magic circle in the air, with your stroke appearing on it live. On your own screen those stay out of the way, so you can see what you're aiming at.
- Every spell is shown by its **sigil**: its shape in its colour on a small dark disc, in the spell row, the Spellbook and the Chest Shop. The disc you are tracing is outlined.
- **Tap a spell card** (or press its key) and that spell's shape appears on screen with a dot showing how to draw it. **Trace it as closely as you can: the better your line follows the shape, the stronger the spell.** A Perfect drawing hits up to 50% harder, up to 25% wider, and lasts up to 30% longer; a Rough one is a bit weaker. With a card selected, drawing a different shape fizzles.
- Draw **big and smooth**. Size, position, and angle don't matter, and you can start anywhere on a closed shape and go either way. A **slash** is just a straight line, in any direction. The X is one unbroken stroke (down, up the side, down).
- Your line glows in the spell's colour with a sparking pen tip. When the spell is cast it flashes in its quality colour (Rough red, Good orange, Great green, Perfect gold) and is pulled into your wand; when the magic couldn't read it, it shakes, turns red, and crumbles. A Great or Perfect drawing also flashes the screen gold. Failures (too small, unknown shape, on cooldown, nowhere to place it) play an error buzz with one short line under the sight.
- Only your loadout's shapes are recognized; the others fizzle.

## Casting

- **First person:** the camera is always first person and every device casts at the sight in the middle of the screen; nobody clicks to cast or aims with the cursor. **Look at your target first, then draw:** the canvas frees the mouse, so the view holds still until you finish. You can still walk while drawing. The sight is one small dot and nothing marks a target. Strikes, lines and walls land exactly where it points. Bolts fly along it and get a small, invisible assist toward the opponent closest to it.
- **Camera speed:** mouse look runs at 25% by default; change it in **Settings**.
- **Getting the mouse:** windows (menus, the Chest Shop, the boost pick, the map vote), the spectate bar and the drawing canvas free the mouse while they're open. Anywhere else (the side menu, the spell cards, Return to Hub), **tap Left Alt**: the cursor stays out until your next click or another tap, then you are back to aiming. Holding **Left Alt** keeps it out until you let go. Touch needs none of this: just tap the button.
- **Ground strikes** (Fire Pillar, Water Nebula, Earth Punch, Lightning, Black Hole) show their hit circle on the ground and a ring for their range limit while you trace them.
- **Placeables** (Earth Wall, Turret) show a green or red ghost while you trace them. They land where you look, at most 20 studs away, and stand side-on to you (**R** turns them further).

## Dash and spectating

- **Dash** is a forward somersault about 28 studs the way you are moving (or facing): arms flung wide, three pale wind streaks behind you, a dust puff and a thud on landing. It needs 8 seconds to recharge (`Config.DASH`). The button shows only the countdown while it recharges.
- **Spectate duels from the hub:** walk to the island's rim (the four SPECTATE overlooks) and look down. Every duel is fought on an arena below the island, with a sign naming the mode and map.
- **Watch** (side menu, in the hub) lists the other players and where they are; pick one to follow them with the camera, and use ◀ ▶ or Stop at the top of the screen. It ends by itself when you join a fight.
- On a phone or tablet, fights hide the menu, wallet, party, kill feed, and boost icons; the spell cards, Draw and Dash buttons, health, and (in the Battleground) Return to Hub stay.

## Settings

The **Settings** panel saves to your profile: **Camera speed**, the draw key, **Background music**, **Sound effects**, and **Screen shake** (camera shake and field-of-view kicks; flashes and sounds stay).

## Accessibility

- UI scales with the screen (`UI/Responsive`). Touch layouts are larger, and buttons are at least 52 px.
- Elements use a name plus a colour, spells use a shape, and chest spells a rarity ring, so nothing depends on colour alone. Text is white on indigo panels and buttons pick dark or white text by their colour (4.5:1 contrast or better).
- **Screen shake** can be switched off in Settings (VR comfort, motion sensitivity).
