# Controls

You get a spell by **drawing its shape**, which adds **one copy** to your hotbar. The shape is the kind of move: circle = a basic attack, a diagonal slash = a fast attack, a squiggly line = movement, a triangle = a stun, a V = a boost or defense, a lightning bolt = a heavy attack, a spiral = an area attack, a star = your ultimate. You can hold up to 5 copies, duplicates included. Casting uses the copy up, and copies of the same spell share one cooldown. Tapping a hotbar slot readies that copy. The key that opens the drawing canvas (default **F**) can be changed in **⚙ Settings**. The setting is saved to your profile, and keys used for movement, chat, the hotbar, canceling (**G**), or rotating can't be chosen. You can draw one spell per second. The glyph cards above the hotbar, the element shrines in the hub, and the Spellbook all show what to draw.

| Action | Keyboard & mouse | Gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation) | Touch (phone / tablet) | VR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open the canvas | **F** (rebindable) or the **✏ Draw** button | **X** | **✏ Draw** button | **F** / **X** |
| Draw the shape | Hold **left click** and drag, release to send | Push the **right stick** around, release to send | Drag a finger, lift to send | Mouse/pointer drag |
| Cancel | **G**, **Esc**, or **F** again | **X** again | Tap **✏ Draw** again | same |
| Ready a held copy | **1–5** | **D-pad ← / →** | Tap a hotbar slot | Hotbar / D-pad |
| Cast | **Left click** (aims at cursor) | **R2** (aims at screen center) | **Tap the world** where you want it | **Right trigger** (aims where your right hand points) |
| Rotate a placeable | **R** | **L1 / R1** | **⟳** button | **L1 / R1** |
| Menus | Side buttons | Select + navigate with the stick | Side buttons | Pointer |

## Drawing tips

- Draw **big and smooth**. Size and angle don't matter, and you can start anywhere on a closed shape and go either way. A **slash** is just a straight line, in any direction.
- The line turns **gold** when the spell is readied and **red** when the magic couldn't read it. Too-small drawings are ignored ("Draw a bigger shape!").
- In a match you can only ready the spells in your spellbook; the others fizzle with a hint.

## Accessibility

- UI scales with the screen. Touch layouts are 15% larger, and buttons are at least 52 px.
- Elements use an icon plus a color (🔥 💧 ⛰ 🌪), and spells use a shape, so nothing depends on color alone.
- No camera shake, which matters for VR comfort.
