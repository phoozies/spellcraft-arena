# Controls

You get a spell by typing (or saying) its word, which puts **one copy** in your hotbar. Casting uses the copy up. Tapping a hotbar slot readies a copy you already hold. You can submit one word per second, and **pasting into the spell box is blocked**: a text change of more than one character at a time is undone. On phones, picking a keyboard autocomplete suggestion counts as a paste, so type the letters instead.

| Action | Keyboard & mouse | Gamepad (Xbox / PlayStation) | Touch (phone / tablet) | VR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Get a spell (type its word) | Type in chat, or press **T** for the spell box | **Y** opens the spell box (on-screen keyboard) | Tap the spell box | **Y** / on-screen keyboard |
| Speak a spell | Hold **🎤** | Hold **🎤** | Hold **🎤** | Hold **🎤** |
| Ready a held copy | **1–5** | **D-pad ← / →** | Tap a hotbar slot | Hotbar / D-pad |
| Cast | **Left click** (aims at cursor) | **R2** (aims at screen center) | **Tap the world** where you want it | **Right trigger** (aims where your right hand points) |
| Rotate a placeable | **R** | **L1 / R1** | **⟳** button | **L1 / R1** |
| Menus | Side buttons | Select + navigate with the stick | Side buttons | Pointer |

## Voice spells

Voice spells need **microphone input** turned on (Game Settings → Communication) and an eligible account. If speech-to-text isn't available, the 🎤 button hides itself. What the game heard appears above the spell box for 2.5 seconds.

## Accessibility

- UI scales with the screen. Touch layouts are 15% larger, and buttons are at least 52 px.
- Rarity uses a shape plus a color (● ◆ ★ ✦), so it's readable without color vision.
- No camera shake, which matters for VR comfort.
