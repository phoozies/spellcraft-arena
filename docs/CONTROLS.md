# Controls

You get a spell by **drawing its shape**, which adds **one copy** to your hotbar. The shape is the kind of move: circle = a basic attack, a diagonal slash = a fast attack, a square = a boost, a triangle = a defense or a stun, a lightning bolt = a heavy attack, a V = an area attack, a star = your ultimate. You hold at most one copy of each spell. Casting uses the copy up. **You can only draw in the Training Grove (hub, west side), during a match's rounds, and in the Battleground**, not elsewhere in the hub and not during the countdown. Tapping a hotbar slot readies that copy. The key that opens the drawing canvas (default **F**) can be changed in **⚙ Settings**. The setting is saved to your profile, and keys used for movement, chat, the hotbar, canceling (**G**), or rotating can't be chosen. You can draw one spell per second. The glyph cards above the hotbar, the element shrines in the hub, and the Spellbook all show what to draw.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draw a spell with a guide | **Click a spell card** above the hotbar | same | **Tap a spell card** | same |
| Open a blank canvas | **F** (rebindable) or the **✏ Draw** button | **X** | **✏ Draw** button | **F** / **X** |

## Drawing tips

- **Tap a spell card** (the row above the hotbar) and that spell's shape appears on screen with a dot showing how to draw it. **Trace it as closely as you can: the better your line follows the shape, the stronger the spell.** The result shows as 1 to 4 stars (Rough, Good, Great, Perfect) on the hotbar copy. A Perfect drawing hits up to 50% harder, up to 25% wider, and lasts up to 30% longer; a Rough one is a bit weaker.
- Start anywhere and go either way; size, position, and angle don't matter for recognition, only the shape does. With a card selected, drawing a different shape fizzles.

- Draw **big and smooth**. Size and angle don't matter, and you can start anywhere on a closed shape and go either way. A **slash** is just a straight line, in any direction.
- The line turns **gold** when the spell is readied and **red** when the magic couldn't read it. Too-small drawings are ignored ("Draw a bigger shape!").
- In a match you can only ready the spells in your spellbook; the others fizzle with a hint.

## Dash and spectating

- **Dash** bursts you about 24 studs the way you are moving (or facing) with a dash animation, then needs 4 seconds to recharge (`Config.DASH`). The button shows the countdown.
- **Watch** (side menu, in the hub) lists the other players and where they are; pick one to follow them with the camera, and use ◀ ▶ or Stop at the top of the screen. It ends by itself when you join a fight.
- On a phone or tablet, fights hide the menu, wallet, party, kill feed, and boost icons; the hotbar, spell cards, Draw and Dash buttons, health, and (in the Battleground) Return to Hub stay.

## Accessibility

- UI scales with the screen. Touch layouts are 15% larger, and buttons are at least 52 px.
- Elements use an icon plus a color (🔥 💧 ⛰ 🌪), and spells use a shape, so nothing depends on color alone.
- No camera shake, which matters for VR comfort.
