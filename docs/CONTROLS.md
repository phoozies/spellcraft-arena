# Controls

You get a spell by **drawing its shape**, which adds **one copy** to your hotbar. The shape is the kind of move: circle = a basic attack, a diagonal slash = a fast attack, a square = a boost, a triangle = a defense or a stun, a lightning bolt = a heavy attack, a V = an area attack, a star = your ultimate; chest spells use a heart, a zigzag, a hook, an X, or a spiral. You hold at most one copy of each spell, and **hotbar slot N always holds the Nth spell of your loadout** (the same order as the spell cards and the 1-5 keys). Casting uses the copy up and leaves its slot empty. **You can only draw in the Training Groves (hub, west and east sides), during a match's rounds, and in the Battleground**, not elsewhere in the hub and not during a match's intro, countdown, draft, or results. The key that opens a blank drawing canvas (default **F**) can be changed in **Settings**. The setting is saved to your profile, and keys used for movement, chat, the hotbar, canceling (**G**), rotating (**R**), dashing (**Q**), or the Admin panel (**`**) can't be chosen. You can draw one spell per second. The spell cards above the hotbar, the element shrines in the hub, and the Spellbook all show what to draw.

## Drawing tips

- **You draw with your wand.** While the canvas is open your character raises the wand and a magic circle opens in the air in front of you; everything you draw on screen is written on that circle as you draw it, and players near you watch it appear live. The canvas darkens only the screen's edges so you can watch it.
- Every spell is shown by its **sigil**: its shape in glowing ink inside a little magic circle of its colour, on the cards, the hotbar, the Spellbook and the Chest Shop. The readied hotbar slot's sigil turns, and so does the card you are tracing.
- **Tap a spell card** (the row above the hotbar) and that spell's shape appears on screen with a dot showing how to draw it. **Trace it as closely as you can: the better your line follows the shape, the stronger the spell.** The result shows as 1 to 4 stars (Rough, Good, Great, Perfect) on the hotbar copy. A Perfect drawing hits up to 50% harder, up to 25% wider, and lasts up to 30% longer; a Rough one is a bit weaker. With a card selected, drawing a different shape fizzles.
- Draw **big and smooth**. Size, position, and angle don't matter, and you can start anywhere on a closed shape and go either way. A **slash** is just a straight line, in any direction. The X is one unbroken stroke (down, up the side, down).
- Your line glows in the spell's colour with a sparking pen tip. When the spell is readied it flashes in its quality colour (Rough red, Good orange, Great green, Perfect gold) and is pulled into your wand; when the magic couldn't read it, it shakes, turns red, and crumbles. A Great or Perfect drawing also flashes the screen gold. Failures (too small, unknown shape, a spell you already hold, on cooldown) play an error buzz instead of a pop-up.
- Only your loadout's shapes are recognized; the others fizzle.

## Casting

- **First person:** the camera is always first person and every device casts at the crosshair in the middle of the screen; nobody aims with the mouse. If an opponent is near the crosshair and in plain sight, the spell locks onto the nearest one (corner brackets show who) and bolts lead and gently track them. The crosshair wears the readied spell's colour and name (red with the seconds left while it recharges).
- **Getting the mouse:** windows (menus, the Chest Shop, the boost pick, the map vote) and the drawing canvas free the mouse while they're open; hold **Left Alt** to use it anywhere else (the side menu, the hotbar).
- **Point-and-click spells** (Fire Pillar, Water Nebula, Earth Punch, Lightning, Black Hole) show their hit circle on the ground and a ring for their range limit.
- **Placeables** (Earth Wall, Turret) show a green or red ghost where they will go.
- The readied hotbar slot pops out with a bubble of the spell's colour.

## Dash and spectating

- **Dash** is a forward somersault about 28 studs the way you are moving (or facing): arms flung wide, three pale wind streaks behind you, a dust puff and a thud on landing. It needs 8 seconds to recharge (`Config.DASH`). The button shows only the countdown while it recharges.
- **Spectate duels from the hub:** walk to the island's rim (the four SPECTATE overlooks) and look down. Every duel is fought on an arena below the island, with a sign naming the mode and map.
- **Watch** (side menu, in the hub) lists the other players and where they are; pick one to follow them with the camera, and use ◀ ▶ or Stop at the top of the screen. It ends by itself when you join a fight.
- On a phone or tablet, fights hide the menu, wallet, party, kill feed, and boost icons; the hotbar, spell cards, Draw and Dash buttons, health, and (in the Battleground) Return to Hub stay.

## Settings

The **Settings** panel saves to your profile: the draw key, **Background music**, **Sound effects**, and **Screen shake** (camera shake and field-of-view kicks; flashes and sounds stay).

## Accessibility

- UI scales with the screen (`UI/Responsive`). Touch layouts are larger, and buttons are at least 52 px.
- Elements use a name plus a colour, spells use a shape, and chest spells a rarity ring, so nothing depends on colour alone. Text is white on indigo panels and buttons pick dark or white text by their colour (4.5:1 contrast or better).
- **Screen shake** can be switched off in Settings (VR comfort, motion sensitivity).
